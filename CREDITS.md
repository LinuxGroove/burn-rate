# Credits

Made by Drew, Kaden, and Ken VanDine, the LinuxGroove team.

## Art

- The tower's offices, labs and server halls, the robots and drones, and
  the props are by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com): the Modular Sci-Fi
  MegaKit and the Sci-Fi Essentials Kit, released under CC0 (public
  domain), in `addons/quaternius/` with their license files.
- The street, the lobby and the city outside are Quaternius' Downtown City
  MegaKit (CC0), in `addons/quaternius/downtowncity/`.
- The pilots and the tower's staff are Quaternius' Universal Base
  Characters with their hair, animated with the Universal Animation
  Library 1 and 2 (CC0), in `assets/people/` with their license files.

Made for Burn Rate, all CC0 like the rest:

- `assets/models/jetpack/`: the six prototype jetpacks, modelled by a
  script (`tools/jetpack.py`) from the MegaKit's trim sheets.
- `assets/models/hazards/` and `assets/models/gear/`: the zapper nodes,
  laser emitters, seekers, goggles and visors, modelled by the Blender
  scripts in `tools/blender/`.
- The beams, flames and glows are shaders in `game/view/fx/`.
- The icon is drawn by `tools/make_icon.py`.

## Fonts

- Kenney Future and Kenney Future Narrow by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), CC0,
  from the shared add-on's `addons/linuxgroove/ui/fonts/`.

## Audio

- `assets/audio/sfx/` and `assets/audio/music/`: every sound, ambience and
  piece of music is synthesized by a script (`tools/sfx/make_sfx.py`), CC0
  like the rest.
- `assets/audio/ui/`: menu sounds from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s
  Interface Sounds, CC0 (see `assets/audio/CREDITS.md`).
